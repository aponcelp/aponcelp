# Hi, I'm Alfredo

I'm an operations leader based in Miami, open to remote roles across the US.

My career has been about helping companies scale efficiently by setting the right data, systems, and process foundations. The work I enjoy most starts with a problem nobody owns yet. I put structure around it, find the root cause, and stay with it until the fix holds.

## How I got here

I started at BMW Group in pricing and product strategy, building the financial models behind vehicle configurations and supporting product launches across markets.

Then I moved to startups. At Clivi, a healthtech company, I was the first product hire and worked next to the founders while the product went from stealth to more than 10,000 monthly users. At Majority, a fintech for immigrants in the US, I ran customer strategy and product operations. We grew the Customer Success team fast and moved most support to self-serve.

Today I'm the GTM Strategy & Operations Manager at DoorLoop, a property management software company with more than $50M in ARR. I work as chief of staff to our customer leadership. While I've been there the CX org grew from 40 to 85 people, and I built Customer Success from scratch for about 15,000 customers. I also build many of the internal tools and AI agents the team runs on.

## Where I'm most useful

- **Business operations and special projects.** Taking a cross-functional problem from a blank page to a plan, a team and a cadence that keep running.
- **Customer and post-sale strategy and operations.** Onboarding, Support and Customer Success: coverage models, capacity, retention, and the systems behind them.
- **Product ops and AI deployment.** Turning a process into an internal app or an agent, deciding what gets automated and what stays with a person, and getting the team to actually use it.
